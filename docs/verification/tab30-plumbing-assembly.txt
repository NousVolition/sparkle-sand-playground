What the nine declarations establish

1. gram_joint_iff — The listed joints are exactly the upper-triangular −1 entries in the simple-root Gram matrix.
2. seven_joints — There are seven distinct joints.
3. distinct_sites — Each attachment site occurs once in the list of paired charts.
4. assembly_connected — Every one of the eight pieces reaches the first piece along the joints.
5. cross_to_listed_sites — Crossing goes to the chart listed at the other end of the selected joint.
6. cross_twice — Crossing a joint in both directions returns the original chart address and disk coordinates.
7. zero_section_to_fiber — The zero section on one side maps to the central fiber on the other.
8. both_zero_sections — Both representatives lie on their zero sections exactly at the chart center.
9. distinct_caps_disjoint — The chosen closed attachment caps around different coordinate axes in S⁴ are disjoint.
