KNOWN TO THE WORLD · The standard positive E₈ plumbing has a rounded boundary homeomorphic to S⁷. Its smooth structure generates Θ₇ ≅ ℤ/28. The plumbing supplies the smooth filling, the identification of its intersection form, and vanishing first Pontryagin class. The Eells–Kuiper theorem identifies its boundary invariant with the formula below. For the standard smooth S⁷, μ is zero.

What Lean checked

1. radial_frontier. In signed squared radii b = |q|² − 1 and f = |p|² − 1, the topological frontier of {b ≤ 0 or f ≤ 0} consists of the two closed rays {b ≥ 0, f = 0} and {b = 0, f ≥ 0}.
2. overlap_boundary_iff. Within the assembly's closed D⁴ × D⁴ chart, the radii lie on that frontier exactly when both disk vectors have unit norm. The seam is modeled by S³ × S³.
3. cross_preserves_seam. Exchanging the two disk factors on the chosen joint preserves membership in this seam.
4. integral_intersection_surjective. The project’s integer E₈ matrix maps ℤ⁸ onto ℤ⁸ via multiplication. Its cokernel is therefore trivial. The plumbing's homology sequence connects this algebraic result to boundary homology.
5. gram_positive_definite. Tab 30’s real Gram matrix is positive definite. The proof uses the coordinates of the eight simple roots and their previously proved Gram determinant.
6. gram_signature. The number of positive eigenvalues minus the number of negative eigenvalues, with multiplicities, is +8. The root Gram form uses the orientation with positive form. The project's integer matrix uses the opposite sign after relabeling.
7. positive_E8_value. For Pontryagin square 0 and signature +8, the rational representative (0 − 4 × 8)/896 is −1/28.
8. positive_E8_order. Using the signature of the actual Tab 30 Gram matrix and square 0, this class has exact additive order 28 in ℚ/ℤ (Lean's AddCircle (1 : ℚ)).
9. positive_E8_nonzero. The class from that Gram signature is nonzero.
10. reversed_orientation_value. For square 0, replacing signature +8 with −8 negates the class, giving +1/28 mod 1.

Geometric scope

YOURS ALONE · The connected overview uses the actual assembly's seven pairs of attachment sites. The seam close-up varies a unit vector q around a great circle in the first S³ while fixing the second unit direction p. In the opposite chart the varying direction is the fiber because the local identification exchanges q and p. The screen shows this circle and a short interval transverse to the rim. The shared ring, angular control, magnification and adjoining-rim navigation visualize the existing assembly and use its checked swap. Selecting another rim on a piece changes the local chart under inspection.

The atlas restricts the base of each unit sphere bundle to a great S² through its attachment centers in S⁴. Over the interior of each used cap its former unit-fiber face lies in the interior after gluing. Outside the caps the unit S³ fibers remain exposed. The corner section shows ordinary radii. The Lean proof uses squared radius minus one, which has the same threshold and side at radius one. Reorienting alternate zero sections along the tree changes the signs of the off-diagonal intersections while preserving the signature. Global smoothing, the identification with a homotopy sphere, the geometric characteristic class and the smooth-invariance theorem enter through the cited mathematics. An end-to-end Lean construction and classification of the smooth boundary remain open in this project.
